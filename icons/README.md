# Icons

Almanac's mark is the analemma: the figure-eight the sun traces across the sky over a year, dotted, white on a sunrise-orange tile.

- `icon.svg`: the source for 48px and up (dotted).
- `icon-16.svg`: the source for 16px, drawn as a solid line because the dots turn to specks at that size.
- `icon16.png`, `icon48.png`, `icon128.png`: rendered from the SVGs by `scripts/icons.sh`. Rerun it after changing either SVG.
